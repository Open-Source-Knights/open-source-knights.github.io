What is Open-Source Knights?

Open-Source Knights is a tabletop roleplaying game framework built from the familiar foundation of D&D 5E, with a greater emphasis on streamlined gameplay, tactical combat, and flexible character design. Players create and customize their own abilities using a shared mechanical language, while the rules are designed to keep those options quick to read, easy to use, and simple to resolve at the table.

Like many TTRPGs, OSK puts the fiction first: players describe what their characters attempt, the Game Master determines how the rules apply, and dice are used when the outcome is uncertain. The goal is to remain approachable to players who already know D&D - or who are completely new to TTRPGs - while giving experienced players much more freedom to build characters that feel uniquely their own.
