What is Open-Source Knights?

Open-Source Knights is a TTRPG framework built on a foundation of D&D 5E. It can be thought of either as a standalone TTRPG, or as a package of rule modifications for overhauling and improving character creation and combat in D&D.

  • Familiar, Easy-to-Learn Gameplay: Based on D&D 5E, but purpose-built with streamlined mechanics that make the game approachable to beginners while enabling maximum creativity for experienced players.
  • Streamlined, Tactical Combat: Combat moves quickly, with clear choices, simple action resolution, and minimal interruption.
  • Ultra-flexible Character Creation: Build characters around your own custom concepts instead of rigid, predefined class packages. You can even "import" abilities and classes from other systems, such as Draw Steel, Daggerheart, or Pathfinder.
  • Player-Created Abilities: Players shape their characters through bespoke Abilities designed in collaboration with the rest of the table, using a shared mechanical language. Customize your Abilities to suit your preferences, whether you want simplicity, narrative flourish, tactical depth, or any combination thereof.
  • Low Cognitive Overhead: A focused, quickly-scannable set of readily available options replaces the need to sift through dozens of options every turn. An Ability "Cooldown Queue" replaces most forms of tedious resource tracking.
  • Intuition Over Exhaustive Rules: Like many TTRPGs, OSK adopts a "rulings, not rules" mindset. Common sense and intuition take priority over having a rule for every possible situation.
